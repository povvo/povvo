### Defects found and repaired, in causal order

1. **Close-on-move snap.** The stage reset one shared extract spring and one shared open spring whenever the selection changed, so an open case snapped shut. Each case now owns gated pull, turn and open springs and reverses its own sequence (`src/gates.ts`).
2. **Cases cutting through each other.**
   - A case turned while still between its neighbours.
   - Filter and sort slid slots past each other.

   Fixed by the gate (turn only once pulled past the rack's clearance) and by the lift reflow.
3. **Snapping in reverse.** The first gate version retracted a case while it was still slightly yawed. The simulation flagged it; measured against the real side gap it was safe, so the invariant was corrected to measure the actual sweep.
4. **Swinging presented case.** A presented case was fixed to its slot, so it swung away sideways with the rack while closing. It now holds the front and goes home along the arc.
5. **Wide sweep on far jumps.** On far jumps, the case's way home swept a wide arc close to the camera. The turn is now phased: it steps back first, then swings home just outside the rack. A new case cannot start pulling out while another is still forward of the arc.
6. **Hue shift.** ACES tone mapping shifted the shells' hues; switched to Neutral.
7. **README blocked in review.** Headless Chromium could not use the container's proxy, so READMEs never loaded in review. External reads are now fetched through `curl` in the review harness only.
8. **Phone callout.** The callout label left the stage on phones. It is hidden below 700 px and flips side for covers whose code sits on the right.

## Defects found and repaired during review, in causal order

1. The page grew to the index's height on wide viewports, so the canvas was taller than the screen. Page height locked; index scrolls inside its column.
2. The spine was a box of the wrong dimensions, so spine labels never showed. Spine rebuilt as a thin slab spanning the case depth.
3. The base yaw was mirrored, so the rack showed each case's open edge. Yaw corrected; extraction now turns the cover to the viewer.
4. Fog range was computed before the camera backed off, fogging near cases. Fog retuned on resize.
5. Selection wrapped around the arc. Clamped.
6. A single ring put the first cases beside the last; a 48 per turn helix stacked rows under the current case. Replaced by an arc that tightens with count, culled by unwrapped angle.
7. Extraction and opening offsets were added in the rack's local frame; with the rack rotated they pointed the wrong way. Offsets rotated into the rack frame.
8. The flat fallback dropped its first render while fonts loaded. Renders coalesced.
9. The rack spring settled to one percent at 0.83 s against the authored half-second intent. Stiffness raised; now 0.64 s.

## What changed from the direction record and why

- The rack is an arc that tightens with the count rather than a helix of 24 per turn: a sparse ring wrapped and a helix stacked rows under the current case; the arc keeps one readable shelf and still becomes a helix past sixty.
- The rack sits left of centre on wide stages and the extracted case comes out to the right, so the caption keeps the lower left and the case keeps the right.
- A soft stock backing sits under the caption on all viewports; the visual-system record had reserved a scrim only for the booklet, and this is a ground role rather than a scrim.
- Hover brightens a spine very slightly, as the direction allowed; nothing else responds to hover.
